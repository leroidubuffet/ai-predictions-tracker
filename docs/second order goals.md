# Second Order Goals

- Accountability by default. Most AI predictions are made, forgotten, and never revisited. A bot that surfaces them at deadline creates social pressure without anyone having to "call out" anyone — the calendar does it.
- Calibration record. Over time, the dataset becomes a credibility map: who makes falsifiable predictions, who hedges, whose track record holds up. That's genuinely useful for evaluating future claims.
- Countering hype cycles. A lot of AI discourse is driven by announcement energy. A steady drip of "here's what was predicted 3 years ago" is a structural counterweight.
- Journalist/researcher resource. A structured YAML archive of sourced, dated predictions is more useful to researchers than scattered tweets and articles.
- Community signal. On Bluesky specifically, the bot could attract followers who want this kind of sober tracking — building an audience that values accountability over hype.
